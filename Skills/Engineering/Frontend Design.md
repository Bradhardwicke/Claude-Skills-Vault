# Frontend Design

Guides creation of distinctive, production-grade frontend interfaces that avoid generic AI-slop aesthetics.

## Design Thinking

Before coding, commit to a BOLD aesthetic direction:
- Pick a tone extreme: brutally minimal, maximalist, retro-futuristic, organic, luxury, playful, editorial, brutalist, art deco, industrial, etc.
- What makes it UNFORGETTABLE?

## Disambiguation

Use over `web-artifacts-builder` for single-file polished UI/components. Use `web-artifacts-builder` for complex multi-component apps with routing/state.
